# Code of Conduct

We want Sourcify to be a welcoming and respectful place for everyone, whatever their background, identity, or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on the problem, not on the person.
- Respect the time of others. Keep issues, comments, and pull requests clear and on topic.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including mass-produced or automated issues, pull requests, and comments.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all Sourcify spaces: the GitHub repositories, Discord, Matrix, and email. It also applies when you represent Sourcify in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently block anyone who doesn't follow this code.

To report a problem, email [hello@sourcify.dev](mailto:hello@sourcify.dev). We review every report and keep the reporter's identity private.

This code is based on ideas from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
